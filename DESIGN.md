## Brand & Style

The design system establishes a high-conversion, agile e-commerce experience for consumer tech, computing, and smart hardware. The aesthetic rejects the cluttered, dark, aggressive "gamer" tropes often associated with technology retail in favor of an airy, luminous, architectural environment with vibrant kinetic accents.

### Core Tenets
- **Commercial Clarity:** Uncompromising visual hierarchy. Pricing, technical specs, inventory indicators, and conversion actions take precedence without visual clutter.
- **Electric Precision:** High-voltage lime accents injected deliberately into a pristine, high-key canvas to signal conversion, new arrivals, and active states.
- **Mobile-First Agility:** Touch targets, bottom sheets, sticky summary drawers, and fluid horizontal discovery rails optimized for single-thumb navigation.
- **Curated Tech Editorial:** Combining crisp geometric typography with precise structural containers to make technology feel accessible, reliable, and premium.

## Colors

The color architecture is built around an ultra-high-key daylight palette punctuated by high-contrast ink and neon-derived chromatic energy.

### Functional Palette
- **Canvas Base (`#ffffff`):** Pure light canvas for clean product presentation and isolated catalog photography.
- **Surface Elevation (`#f8f9fa`, `#f1f3f5`):** Subtly tinted cool grays used to establish component cards, segmented filter bars, and background modules.
- **Primary CTA (`#9ec915`):** Specially calibrated electric lime engineered to pass AA contrast thresholds against white or light gray surfaces for badges, borders, and active indicators.
- **Accent Highlight (`#c6f135`):** Ultra-bright kinetic lime reserved for high-impact hero ribbons, discount callouts, and fill states paired strictly with `#111827` ink typography.
- **Deep Ink (`#111827`):** Deep obsidian gray used for high-impact primary buttons, critical data points, and primary headers.
- **Muted Slate (`#64748b`):** Balanced neutral for technical specifications, inactive states, secondary labels, and icon fills.
- **Hairline Border (`#e2e8f0`):** Ultra-soft edge definition separating modular grid cells without visual heaviness.

## Typography

The type system pairs the technical, confident geometry of **Space Grotesk** for numerical data, headlines, and navigation labels with the structural legibility of **Inter** for dense specs, descriptions, and checkout workflows.

### Hierarchy & Treatment
- **Display & Headlines:** Space Grotesk in bold cuts with negative tracking. Used for product banners, campaign titles, and bold pricing headers.
- **Price Presentation:** Numerical prices are rendered in Space Grotesk SemiBold or Bold with currency symbols superscaled or styled as `label-md`.
- **Specs & Microcopy:** Inter Regular and Medium for spec sheets, product comparison tables, tooltips, and delivery badges.
- **Badges & Tags:** Space Grotesk in uppercase with tracked spacing (`letterSpacing: 0.04em` to `0.06em`) for promotional pills, stock markers, and spec callouts.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is engineered from the viewport up using a fluid grid system tuned for high-density e-commerce browsing.

### Grid & Breakpoint Strategy
- **Mobile (320px – 639px):** 4-column layout. 16px lateral margin, 12px gutter. Product grids default to 2 columns with aspect-ratio locked media cards.
- **Tablet (640px – 1023px):** 8-column layout. 24px lateral margin, 16px gutter. Product grids reflow into 3 columns; filter panels tuck into responsive flyouts.
- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12-column layout capped at a maximum container width of `1280px`. 40px margins, 24px gutters. Dual-pane checkout and 4-column catalog feeds.

### Touch & Rhythm Principles
- All mobile interactive targets maintain a mandatory minimum dimension of 44x44px.
- Product card stacks use an 8px vertical internal card cadence, while outer section modules utilize 32px on mobile and 64px on desktop.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses a luminous surface-layering philosophy, avoiding heavy, muddy drop shadows in favor of crisp outlines and airy ambient occlusion.

### Elevation Levels
- **Base Surface (Level 0):** Pure `#ffffff` or `#f8f9fa` canvas without shadow.
- **Surface Cards (Level 1):** `#ffffff` layered over `#f8f9fa` with a 1px border (`#e2e8f0`) and soft ambient bloom: `0 2px 8px -2px rgba(17, 24, 39, 0.04), 0 1px 4px -1px rgba(17, 24, 39, 0.02)`.
- **Active / Hover State (Level 2):** Subtle lift for hovered cards and active product tiles: `0 12px 24px -6px rgba(17, 24, 39, 0.07), 0 4px 8px -2px rgba(17, 24, 39, 0.03)` with border shifting to `#cbd5e1`.
- **Sticky Bars & Flyouts (Level 3):** Used for mobile bottom navigation, sticky Add-to-Cart rails, and cart slide-overs: `0 -4px 20px rgba(17, 24, 39, 0.06)` with backdrop blur filter `blur(12px)` over a 90% opaque background.
- **Modals & Overlays (Level 4):** Dialog windows, zoom viewers, and image galleries: `0 24px 48px -12px rgba(17, 24, 39, 0.15)` with backdrop scrim `#111827` at 40% opacity.

## Shapes

The design system employs modern, welcoming contours while preserving clean, engineered geometry.

### Radius Assignments
- **Core Elements (0.5rem / 8px):** Input textboxes, dropdown select triggers, and inline tech specification chips.
- **Interactive Controls (0.75rem / 12px):** Standard buttons, secondary triggers, segmented control pills, and notification toasts.
- **Structural Containers (1rem to 1.5rem / 16px to 24px):** Product cards, promo banners, cart summary boxes, and modal panels.
- **Pill Geometry (9999px):** Promo highlight badges, stock indicators, filter chips, and floating cart badge counters.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary CTA:** Background `#111827`, text `#ffffff`, font `Space Grotesk 600`. Hover shifts to `#1f2937` with subtle translateY(-1px). For direct "Buy Now" conversion triggers, an alternative primary option uses `#c6f135` background with `#111827` text.
- **Secondary CTA:** Background `#ffffff`, border 1px solid `#e2e8f0`, text `#111827`. Hover shifts to background `#f8f9fa` with border `#cbd5e1`.
- **Ghost/Tertiary:** Background transparent, text `#111827`, hover background `#f1f3f5`.
- **Mobile Sticky CTA:** Fixed bottom dock, 100% width button with price split (left: price/subtotal in Inter Bold; right: action label with arrow icon).

### Cards
- **Product Card:** Elevated on `#ffffff` with a 1px `#e2e8f0` border. Features an aspect-ratio locked image stage with `#f8f9fa` background, top-right wishlist icon button, top-left promotional pill (`#c6f135`), category micro-label, 2-line clamped title in Space Grotesk, price display, and instant Add-to-Cart tap target.
- **Specification Mini-Card:** `#f8f9fa` background, no shadow, 12px padding, displaying technical attribute (e.g., "RAM", "16GB") in stacked monospace/geometric alignment.

### Chips & Badges
- **Discount Badge:** Solid `#c6f135` pill with `#111827` bold text.
- **Stock Status Badge:** Green dot indicator (`#9ec915`) accompanied by `In Stock` label in Slate 700; low stock triggers warning amber dot.
- **Filter Chips:** Inactive: background `#ffffff`, border `#e2e8f0`, text `#64748b`. Active: background `#111827`, border `#111827`, text `#ffffff`.

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Text Inputs:** Height 48px (mobile touch target), background `#ffffff`, border 1px solid `#e2e8f0`, radius 10px, text `#111827`. Focus state reveals a 2px outer ring in `#9ec915` with neutral border.
- **Search Bar:** Integrated glass-style or elevated search with quick-clear button, leading search icon, and auto-suggest overlay featuring instant product thumbnails and SKU numbers.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Custom 20x20px controls with 4px border radius. Checked state fills `#111827` or `#9ec915` with pure white checkmark icon.

### Commerce Specifics
- **Quantity Selector:** Pill container with recessed minus/plus buttons (`#f1f3f5`) flanking a centered bold numerical counter.
- **Bottom Navigation Bar (Mobile):** 60px height, 5 icons (Home, Categories, Search, Wishlist, Cart with dynamic counter pill in `#c6f135`).
