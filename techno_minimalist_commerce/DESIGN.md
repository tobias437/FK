## Brand & Style

The design system establishes a high-precision, technical, and dependable e-commerce experience. It merges dark-mode minimalism with utility-driven tech aesthetics to project authority, speed, and uncompromising reliability. The primary brand mark **FK** stands sharp, bold, and architectural, supported by the pragmatic descriptor *"tu tienda de soluciones"*.

### Aesthetic Foundation
- **Style Archetype:** Modern Technical Minimalism infused with functional Dark Mode engineering.
- **Visual Tenor:** Monolithic dark planes, low-contrast razor-sharp structural dividers, and deliberate high-voltage lime accents reserved strictly for focal conversions.
- **Emotional Impact:** Professionalism, frictionless utility, digital precision, and absolute trust across both merchandise retail and specialized technical hardware services.

### Core Paradigms
- **Purposeful Contrast:** Dark charcoal canvases create immediate depth and silence visual noise; crisp white conveys typographic legibility, while electric lime activates decisive user actions.
- **Service & Product Balance:** Balanced architectural treatment supporting both consumer merchandise catalogs and analytical technical service diagnostic intake flows.

## Colors

The color architecture enforces strict hierarchy across high-density catalog environments, eliminating cognitive overload through utilitarian discipline.

### Palette Roles
- **Primary Accent (`#C6F135`, hover: `#B4E61F`):** Technical Electric Lime. Applied exclusively to high-intent triggers: Add-to-Cart buttons, express checkout triggers, diagnostic submission buttons, urgent badges, and active checkout milestones. Never applied to large static background areas.
- **Base Canvas (`#090D12`):** Ultra-dark charcoal foundation for full-bleed page depth.
- **Surface Layer 1 (`#0D1117`):** Primary viewport container, cards, and default canvas elements.
- **Surface Layer 2 (`#161B22`):** Elevated interactive tiles, sliding side-cart panel, flyouts, and modal viewports.
- **Surface Layer 3 (`#21262D`):** Input field fills, hover states on table rows, and secondary utility chips.
- **Borders & Dividers (`#30363D`):** Precise single-pixel ghost borders defining component architecture.
- **Muted Text (`#8B949E`):** Secondary metadata, specifications, deactivated states, and breadcrumbs.
- **Primary Text (`#FFFFFF`):** High-contrast headlines, prices, primary labels, and product titles.
- **Tertiary System (`#2EA043` / `#25D366`):** Dedicated direct WhatsApp Business communication actions and verified hardware diagnostic status indicators.

## Typography

Typography reinforces high-speed scanability and technical rigor through a dual-font pairing:

- **Display & Headings (Space Grotesk):** Engineered geometry with subtle algorithmic nuances. Used for the prominent brand wordmark **FK**, collection headers, catalog badges, pricing amounts, and diagnostic service tracking tags.
- **Body & Controls (Inter):** Highly legible grotesque sans-serif with tall x-height and neutral character structures, ensuring effortless readability for product descriptions, service SLAs, technical hardware specifications, and data inputs.
- **Case Rules:** All micro-labels, category indicators, badges, and technical specs use uppercase styling with positive letter-spacing (`0.04em` to `0.06em`) for immediate parsing.

## Layout & Spacing

The design system operates on an exact 8pt grid with a 4pt sub-grid for precise visual alignments (icon optical adjustments, badge insets, tabular numeric data).

### Layout System
- **Grid Architecture:** 12-column fluid grid on desktop (`>1024px`) with `24px` gutters and dynamic margin padding up to a maximum container constraint of `1280px`. 6-column layout on tablet (`768px - 1023px`), and 4-column layout on mobile (`<767px`) with `16px` gutters.
- **Section Rhythm:** Major page modules (Hero Showcase, 7 Category Rail, Technical Service Booking Terminal, Flash Offers) maintain `64px` vertical spacing on desktop and `40px` on mobile screens.
- **Utility Flyouts:** Side-cart (Drawer) occupies a strict `420px` width on desktop and `100vw` on mobile devices with zero-gap docking on the right edge.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system rejects heavy, fuzzy drop shadows in favor of **structural containment, low-contrast ghost outlines, and subtle luminous underglows**.

### Depth Layers
- **Tier 0 (Floor Canvas):** Flat `#090D12` without border or shadow.
- **Tier 1 (Surface / Standard Cards):** `#0D1117` encapsulated by a 1px continuous border of `#30363D`. No drop shadow.
- **Tier 2 (Elevated Panels & Dropdowns):** `#161B22` framed by a 1px border of `#30363D`, supported by a dark ambient drop: `0px 12px 32px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.65)`.
- **Tier 3 (Modals & Slide-Out Cart):** `#161B22` anchored with a high-depth structural occlusion shadow: `0px 24px 64px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.85)` accompanied by a left-side 1px border of `#30363D`.
- **Active Focus & Hover Glow:** High-priority elements use a targeted focal perimeter glow instead of blur: `0px 0px 0px 1px #C6F135, 0px 0px 16px rgba(198, 241, 53, 0.25)`.

## Shapes

The shape system adopts crisp, technical, restrained rounding (`roundedness: 1`). It evokes precision-tooled hardware rather than soft toy-like surfaces.

- **Micro Controls & Badges (`rounded-sm`):** `2px` to `4px` corner radii for category indicators, serial number tags, count pills, and technical specs.
- **Buttons, Inputs & Tiles (`rounded`):** `4px` corner radius. Maintains industrial stability and clean edge alignment across tightly nested data grids.
- **Drawers, Floating Bars & Panels (`rounded-md` / `rounded-lg`):** `8px` corner radius strictly for modal viewports and floating toast confirmations.
- **Pill Exceptions:** Rounded-full is permitted solely for numeric quantity badges in the cart icon and quick category filter tags to communicate swipeable interactivity.

## Components

### 1. Brand Header & Navigation
- **Mark Display:** Monolithic bold `FK` in Space Grotesk (`fontSize: 24px`, bold uppercase) paired with the secondary descriptor `"tu tienda de soluciones"` in Inter (`fontSize: 11px`, `#8B949E`, letter-spaced).
- **Category Rail:** Direct links to the 7 core pillars: *Hogar*, *Moda*, *Tecnología*, *Accesorios*, *Móvil*, *Juguetes*, *Cuidados y Belleza*. Horizontal scroller on mobile with subtle border separators.
- **Technical Service Tab:** High-contrast dedicated badge button labeled *"Servicio Técnico"* featuring an active wrench icon and subtle green status dot (`#2EA043`) signaling workshop availability.

### 2. Buttons
- **Primary CTA (Add-to-Cart / Express Checkout):** Solid `#C6F135` background with pure black `#090D12` bold typography. Hover translates smoothly into `#B4E61F` with slight forward scale transform (1.01x).
- **Secondary Action:** Transparent background, 1px `#30363D` border, `#FFFFFF` text. Hover shifts border to `#8B949E` and background to `#161B22`.
- **WhatsApp Direct Action:** Solid `#25D366` icon button with dark `#090D12` text, offering instant support access or direct quote confirmation for phone repairs.

### 3. Product & Solution Cards
- **Structure:** Background `#0D1117`, 1px border `#30363D`, with `4px` radius.
- **Image Viewport:** High-contrast gray frame `#161B22` with seamless edge-to-edge product render. Quick-add micro-interaction appears on hover via an overlay button.
- **Data Cluster:** Category pill in Space Grotesk (`10px`, uppercase, `#8B949E`), clear 2-line title in `#FFFFFF`, and high-impact price display in bold Space Grotesk.

### 4. Technical Service Terminal Card (Phone Repair Module)
- **Specialized Card:** Features model selector (Brand, Device, Fault: Screen, Battery, Port, Logic Board), estimated turnaround SLA badge, instant repair quote preview, and direct "Iniciar Reparación por WhatsApp" conversion action.

### 5. Flyout Cart (Side-Cart Drawer)
- **Mechanics:** Slides from the right edge over a darkened backdrop blur (`rgba(9, 13, 18, 0.75)` backdrop filter `blur(4px)`).
- **Contents:** Free-shipping progress track in `#C6F135`, concise cart list with quantity steppers (`- 1 +`), instant promo field, and a persistent double CTA: "Checkout Express" (Primary Lime) and "Comprar directo por WhatsApp" (Secondary Green).

### 6. Inputs & Express Form Fields
- **Container:** Background `#161B22`, border 1px `#30363D`, text `#FFFFFF`, placeholder `#8B949E`.
- **Focus:** 1px border `#C6F135` and subtle outer lime glow without UI shift.
